2. DESCRIPTION OF SERVICE

The Daily Intelligence Brief is a personal productivity tool that performs 
the following functions:

  - Aggregates data from the user's personal Up Bank account (financial 
    transactions), Notion workspace (tasks, goals, frameworks), and Oura 
    Ring account (sleep, readiness, and activity metrics).
  - Transmits aggregated data to OpenAI's API to generate natural language 
    intelligence briefs that synthesize financial, productivity, and wellness 
    information.
  - Saves generated daily and weekly briefs to the user's personal Notion 
    workspace for review and reference.

The Application is designed to provide the user with a holistic, AI-generated 
overview of their daily life across financial, task management, and health 
domains, enabling more informed personal decision-making.
